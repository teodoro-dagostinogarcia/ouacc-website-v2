Historical material from the Club archive.

Archive record. Original historical or technical material is retained here for reference.
Historical material is retained as part of the Club archive. Contemporary summaries may be edited, but original records and results are preserved.

The Croquet Association, the national governing body for the sport in England and Wales, is pleased to sponsor the Croquet Cuppers and delighted that so many have taken part. Many members of Oxford University Association Croquet Club have gone on to play in the higher levels of competition including several who have represented their countries in international teams.

To find the nearest croquet club to your home, where you will be able to get more coaching and competitive play, go to www.croquet.org.uk/clubs/ .

The 2012 seeded teams, based on last years competition, are (in order from 1st seed to 8th seed): St Edmund Hall 1, Trinity MCR 1, Keble 1, Pembroke 1, Oriel 1, New 2, ST Benet's 1, Exeter 1

To find your team, search by pressing ctrl-F (or cmd-F on a mac) and enter the email of your team captain.
