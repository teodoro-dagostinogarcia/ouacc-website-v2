Cuppers results

How to report Cuppers results and request extensions or reviews.

Please report each result promptly so the draw can be updated and the competition can proceed on schedule.

Results should be emailed promptly to the Cuppers Director to allow the competition to progress swiftly.

Please include the information indicated below. Email templates are given to save you typing too much.

1). RESULTS: email template

Please include:

  • Your Oxford email address (if different from where you are sending it)

  • Winning Team ID (e.g. bras2)

  • Losing Team ID (e.g. magd11)

  • Score and/or Additional Comments

2) EXTENSION REQUESTS: email template

Please include:

  • Your Oxford email address (if different from where you are sending it)

  • Your Team ID (e.g. bras2)

  • Opponents' Team ID (e.g. magd11)

  • Date Requested (e.g. Tuesday of 5th May)

  • Reason for Extension

2) DISPUTE REVIEW: email template

If you and the team you are currently playing have a dispute that needs reviewing by the Cuppers Director.

Please include:

  • Your Oxford email address (if different from where you are sending it)

  • Your Team ID (e.g. bras2)

  • Opponents' Team ID (e.g. magd11)

  • Reason for Dispute

If the templates are not working for you, please email the Cuppers Director ( 2026croquetcuppers@gmail.com ) directly.

Croquet Cuppers 2026 is supported by Sipsmith, our Official Drinks Partner. Founded in London in 2009, Sipsmith was the first copper-pot distillery to open in the city in nearly two centuries, playing a significant role in the revival of small-batch gin production in the UK. The distillery produces its spirits using traditional copper stills and a one-shot distillation process. Its core product, Sipsmith London Dry Gin, is made using a blend of botanicals including juniper berries, citrus peels, and spices, alongside a broader range of expressions such as sloe gin, overproof gin, and flavoured variants. Sipsmith has become an established name in the modern craft spirits sector, combining traditional distillation methods with a contemporary product range, and its support contributes to the delivery of Croquet Cuppers 2026, including the competition finals.
