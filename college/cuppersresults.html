Cuppers

Cuppers results

Report results, request an extension, or ask the Cuppers Director to review a dispute.

On this page
Report a result Request an extension Request a review Contact the Cuppers Director

Please report each match result promptly so the draw can be updated and the competition can continue on schedule.

Report a result

Email the Cuppers Director with the information below.

  • Your Oxford email address, if different from the address you are using.
  • Winning team ID.
  • Losing team ID.
  • Score and any relevant comments.

Email the result

Request an extension

Ask for an extension before the existing deadline. Include your team ID, your opponents' team ID, the date requested, and the reason for the request.

Request an extension

Request a review

If a dispute requires a decision by the Cuppers Director, email the Director with your team ID, your opponents' team ID, and a concise explanation of the issue.

Request a review

Contact the Cuppers Director

For any other Cuppers question, email 2026croquetcuppers@gmail.com.
