Cuppers FAQ

Answers to common questions about Croquet Cuppers.

  1. What should we do if our college doesn't have a court?
    • Play both games at your opponent's courts or use another college's courts.
  2. What happens if the ball hits the peg?
    • If the ball has not been through all twelve hoops, nothing happens - the game does not reset. See Law 22 for further details.
  3. Which way does the ball go through the final hoop?
    • Away from the peg. See diagram above Law 1 .
  4. Where does the game start?
    • The game starts from either baulk-line - not a mallet's length from the first hoop. See Law 11 .
  5. When has a ball run a hoop?
    • When the ball clears the plane of the playing side of the hoop and comes to rest. See Law 20 .
  6. What happens if both teams have equal points after both games? E.g. 26-18 & 18-26
    • You may choose to play a deciding game with any two players from your team or conduct a coin flip.
