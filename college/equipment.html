Where to Buy Equipment

[Updated April 2026.]

The most economic way of buying equipment is to buy the best value pieces of kit separately. Boxed sets are mostly naff and include unnecessary items. Separate pieces are available from various shops, but for many items the Croquet England Shop offers better prices than the high-street. Contact the CqE shop on 01242 233 555 or email office@croquetengland.org.uk . Note that being an U25 Premium Croquet England Member (£10.50/£18.00) gives you a 10% member discount on many item in the CqE Shop, so if buying a George Wood mallet (£265-374), it is cheaper to join and buy a mallet with discount than buy a mallet on its own, etc. All you have to do is persuade your college/JCR to buy into the idea! Do check that the sum remains correct and exactly which items and how many of each are discounted in your membership deal!

Boxed Croquet Sets from the Croquet England Shop

The one thing not to compromise on is the ball weight.

Discounts: If someone joins Croquet England as a premium member, e.g. £10.50 or £18 for an under 26-year old, there is a 10% discount on all items from the shop. Hence the George Wood Hurlingham 4-Player Croquet Set at £528.00 gets a £52.80 discount easily covering the membership.

The shop manager also writes: " If a college is looking to buy a lot of stock, they are welcome to contact us directly and we can look at what we can do for them as well. " [ office@croquetengland.org.uk ].

Suitable Equipment for College Use

The sections below contain details of items suitable for college use, and which are commonly used at the OUACC. For detailed information about buying kit see https://oxfordcroquet.org/equip . The prices in the sections below are only given for guidance: for up-to-date prices, please contact the supplier.

Balls

Note that for cuppers, visiting teams are permitted to bring standard equipment to substituted for absent or inferior equipment! You do not want to be practicing with a garage forecourt set. There are currently 3 types of Croquet England approved balls you can purchase. These meet the required tight specifications (size, bounce, weight). Prices per set of four balls.

  • Sunshiny CQ-16 16 oz Tournament
    £491 available from the Croquet England Shop . These are more suitable for college purchases given their lower price. These are approved by Croquet England for championship play.
  • Invictus Balls . Due soon in the UK.
    £753! These are premium championship standard croquet balls available from the Croquet England Shop and approved by Croquet England for championship play. The price is however ridiculous.
Available only from Australia - think long delivery times, shipping cost and customs duty:
  • Dawson International Mk3
    Priced at around £550 for a set of four balls: https://www.dawsonballs.store/ . Croquet England approved for championship play.

Many other balls DO NOT MEET the CqE specifications . If you have to economise ensure that the balls weigh 16 oz (454 g). Read the description carefully: " The balls meet with the regulation specifications for size and weight ". Urgh !, they do not meet the bounce specifications and are not approved.

Mallets

When buying mallets there are several factors to consider, as well as price. (See Oxford Croquet for more than you wanted to know):

  • Handle length
    The most common length is 36" (~91 cm), but if you are buying several for a college, it may be useful to include one that is slightly longer, for example 38" (96.5 cm), which may be more comfortable for taller players. Conversely, a shorter one can be useful, although this is less critical since players can always hold their hands slightly lower down a long shafted mallet.
  • Handle material
    Although mallets with wooden shafts are sometimes cheaper than other types, for general use in a college it IS worth spending a little extra to buy a mallet with a carbon fibre or metal shaft which will not break easily if it receives rough treatment from inexperienced players. Having replacement handles/shafts fitted is expensive.
  • Weight
    The typical weight is approximately 3lb (1360 g), since this often provides the best compromise for the demands of different strokes. While handle length is a common option on mallets you normally accept the provided weight unless you want a bespoke mallet.
  • Head length
    Basic mallets often have approximately 9" long heads. However, where a longer head-length is an available option, it is well worth considering since longer heads are more 'stable' and more accurate to play with. For this reason the mallets used by elite players all tend to be about 11" long. Bear in mind also that longer length heads are easier to catch on the ground as the mallet is swung - for this reason for college use it is not advisable to buy a mallet with a head that is longer than 11". 10 - 11" is fine.
  • Head width
    Narrower heads enable some shots to be played that might be hampered by a wider head, and most more expensive mallets tend to be quite narrow. However, for inexperienced players, such as for general college use, it is advisable to have a mallet with a standard head to reduce the likelihood of miss-hitting.

The following is a selection of mallets of interest.

If possible avoid mallets with wooden shafts - they have a half-life measured in days. Pay extra for a carbon fibre shaft.

  • George Wood - Hybrid, Evolution or Discovery Mallets (£265-374) - available from the CqE Shop (Note potential 10% discount for Croquet England Premium members). Straightforward to purchase.
  • Manor House's "2000" (~£285) carbon fibre shaft, mallet wooden heads. Longer heads are available. Supplied by the manufacturer " Dave Trimmer ". If you have a silly budget the top of the range carbon fibre mallets come in at ~£500! There can be a waiting list of MANY months for some mallets so order early.
  • db Mallets . UK manufacturer. Robust design. £248 - 336.
  • M.C. Percival Mallets - www.croquetmallets.co.uk .
  • PHC mallets from South Africa - rapid delivery.

Don't forget that there are also charges for postage. If you buy several mallets, where applicable, postage is charged at bulk rates. Beware that mallets ordered from abroad can attract taxes and duties as well as shipping costs.

Hoops

The ideal hoops for college are heavy metal hoops. Currently these would include the Croquet England Championship Croquet Hoops (£354) or if you have very deep pockets the Ultra Championship Croquet Hoops (£660!) If your college buys solid metal hoops they should last many years. Tube and wire hoops are poor substitutes but budgets can be limited. See available hoops on the Croquet England Shop site. If possible avoid hoops which are 4" (10.1 cm) wide; the intended gape is 3.75" (~9.5 cm).
